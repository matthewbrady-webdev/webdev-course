My Top Ten Video Games

Top 3

  1. Minecraft

    2. The reason Minecraft earns the top spot is mostly that it is nostalgic, and you can do something new everytime you play. It also can be enjoyed in a group or by yourself.

  2. The Legned of Zelda Franchise

    3. Yes, i know this is more than 1 game, but i put it here because it is a classic game with an entertaining story line that i could play for hours on end.

    Games 4 - 6

  4. Terraria

  9. Super Smash Bros.

    10. A very enjoyable multiplayer combat game (where i always win) that can be played with the whole family.

  10. NBA 2K

    11. My favorite sports game that i enjoy playing with family and friends.
